What this actually costs, and what your kid's learning

A one-page case for why this build is worth considering next to a week of summer camp — real numbers from our own shopping list, not marketing copy.

The cost, honestly

Every number below is computed straight from the shopping list's real prices and quantities — nothing rounded up for effect.

Building one car
~$619
Every fastener, bearing, electronic, and consumable on the real shopping list, priced at what we actually paid in 2026, for one car. CURV LAB's own guide separately estimates ~$600 in hardware for one build — close enough to trust both numbers.
STEM / robotics camp, one week
$170–$1,200
Real range we found across 2026 day-camp listings — budget local programs run as low as ~$170–$300/week, name-brand tech camps run $500–$1,200/week; overnight programs start around $950/week and can run past $3,000. Wherever a given camp lands, your kid keeps a week of memories. They don't keep a robotics camp.
What's not in this number: a 3D printer (we already owned a Bambu X1C — budget for one if you don't), PETG-HF filament (~365g, a few dollars' worth), and CURV LAB's own print files (bought once from their store, not tracked on our shopping list).
